About Us

Amaze Academy is a modern educational institution leveraging technology to monitor and enhance student performance. We serve learners from Grade 1 through Grade 12, offering a complete pathway from foundational literacy and numeracy to college-preparatory coursework.

Our approach blends strong academic standards with personalised learning: we use diagnostic assessments, adaptive learning modules, and teacher-led interventions so every student progresses at the right pace.

Facilities include modern classrooms, a resource library, computer labs, and safe campus amenities. We also run extracurricular clubs and community projects that build leadership and practical skills.

Join Amaze Academy to give students a balanced education that prepares them academically and personally for the future.
